## About

I got into coding because I wanted to know what was actually
happening behind the screen.

Started with C++, got pulled into DSA, and then somehow college
projects, GitHub, LeetCode, and random debugging sessions became
part of the routine.

I like the part where something doesn't work.

Not immediately, obviously.

But there's something satisfying about staring at a problem for
way too long, finding the stupid little thing you missed, and
finally seeing it work.

Most of the stuff here is just proof that I was there —
solved this, built that, broke something, fixed something,
learned something.

Still a work in progress.
